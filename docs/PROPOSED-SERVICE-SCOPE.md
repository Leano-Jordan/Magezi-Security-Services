# Magezi Security Services — Proposed Service Scope

**Status:** Partner-proposed service interests recorded on 2026-10-20. This is not an approved service catalogue, operating plan, or confirmation of legal authority, insurance, staffing, funding, or customer demand.

## 1. What Kabelo said he wants to offer

Kabelo identified these six areas:

1. VIP protection
2. Guard patrol
3. Armed response
4. Risk management
5. Tactical response
6. Event security

This records Kabelo's stated interests only. It does not mean that Rosco or Kabelo has agreed to launch all six, or that Magezi can currently provide any of them.

## 2. Recommended launch approach

Magezi is starting with no confirmed customers and no established startup budget. Keep the first offer narrow enough to validate demand, cost it properly, and deliver reliably.

### First services to investigate

- **Contract guarding:** Continue investigating guarding for businesses and properties as the initial business hypothesis.
- **Event security:** Test whether there is demand for occasional event assignments, but only after confirming the relevant requirements and ability to deliver.
- **Risk assessments:** Define what Kabelo means by “risk management” and whether he has the appropriate experience and qualifications to provide the specific service proposed.

These are research priorities, not permission to advertise or deliver services.

### Defer pending evidence and professional checks

- **VIP protection**
- **Armed response**
- **Tactical response**

Do not market these as available or accept work until the exact service has been defined and the relevant regulatory position, qualified personnel, insurance, equipment, operating capacity, funding, and safe service-delivery arrangements have been independently confirmed. No operational tactics or weapons-related procedures belong in this planning document.

## 3. Questions for Kabelo

Discuss these questions together before finalising the service catalogue:

- Which one service does he want Magezi to sell first, and why?
- What relevant experience, qualifications, registrations, and verifiable references does he have for each proposed service?
- Which service can Magezi deliver with the people, funding, and resources actually available?
- Who would buy it, who makes the buying decision, and what evidence would a customer require from a new provider?
- What are the complete startup and monthly costs, including staffing, supervision, transport, insurance, compliance, and working capital?
- What services would require specialist staff or external partners rather than Magezi delivering them directly?
- What restrictions or conflicts may arise from his current employment?

Record answers as confirmed facts only when supported by evidence. Keep estimates and unresolved items clearly labelled.

## 4. Compliance and trust gate

Before offering any security service for payment:

- Confirm current business and individual registration requirements directly with PSiRA.
- Verify relevant qualifications and registration status for the people who would perform and manage the service.
- Obtain appropriate professional advice on service-specific legal requirements, insurance, employment obligations, contracts, privacy, and liability.
- Confirm that staffing, supervision, communications, equipment, funding, and incident-management arrangements are sufficient for the promised service.
- Do not claim Magezi is registered, authorised, insured, staffed, or operational unless that status has been verified.

Official starting points:

Requirements vary by service and operating model. The links are starting points, not a compliance determination.

## 5. Commercial decision rule

Choose the first service based on evidence, not how impressive it sounds:

1. A real, reachable customer segment exists.
2. Customers have a clear need and a credible buying process.
3. Magezi can meet the relevant requirements.
4. Full delivery costs and working capital are understood.
5. The founders agree who is responsible and how the work will be funded.
6. The service can be delivered reliably without making promises the business cannot keep.

## 6. Current decision

**No expanded service catalogue has been approved.** Keep contract guarding for businesses and properties as the current initial hypothesis. Use Kabelo's list to guide a focused discussion and feasibility checks; update the business plan only when the service choice is supported by evidence.
