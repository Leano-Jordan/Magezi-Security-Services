# Magezi Security Services — Initial Action Plan

**Status:** Proposed actions; owners and deadlines must be confirmed.  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20  
**Operating rule:** Business planning first; software development only when a validated need exists.

## Priority 1 — Founder alignment

- [ ] Arrange a focused discussion with the prospective operating partner.
- [ ] Confirm the services he wants to offer first and what he can personally deliver.
- [ ] List each founder's cash, time, skills, equipment, relationships and ongoing commitments.
- [ ] Clarify whether Rosco's role is ownership, paid services, or a combination.
- [ ] Agree not to incur significant costs or undertake unlimited unpaid work until basic terms are written down.
- [ ] Check the prospective partner's current employment obligations before taking steps that could create a conflict.

**Output:** A short written record of shared goals, contributions, unresolved issues and the next decision.

## Priority 2 — Compliance and structure

- [ ] Obtain the current PSiRA business-registration checklist directly from PSiRA.
- [ ] Verify the prospective operating partner's individual registration, grade, standing and relevant qualifications.
- [ ] Confirm whether proposed directors and people performing executive/management functions must meet specific PSiRA registration requirements.
- [ ] Before deciding whether Rosco should be a director, confirm the regulatory implications of that role. Do not assume shareholding and directorship are interchangeable.
- [ ] Confirm CIPC, SARS, payroll, UIF, COIDA, insurance, employment and premises requirements relevant to the intended model.
- [ ] Check whether the business can meet requirements within the available budget before committing to registration or other spending.

**Output:** A verified compliance checklist with estimated costs, evidence needed, owner and status for each item.

## Priority 3 — Customer validation

- [ ] Select one initial customer segment after discussing the operating partner's experience and capacity.
- [ ] Build a prospect list of local businesses/property decision-makers; use public business contact channels.
- [ ] Speak to prospective buyers to learn their current guarding needs, contract expectations, procurement requirements and decision process.
- [ ] Do not imply that Magezi is already licensed, insured or operational if that has not been verified.
- [ ] Record each conversation, evidence, objections, next step and follow-up date.
- [ ] Identify the minimum credible requirements for a first contract.

**Output:** Evidence of demand, a prioritised prospect list and a clear view of the first target service.

## Priority 4 — Contract economics and operating capacity

- [ ] Define the exact duties and service hours for the target contract type.
- [ ] Estimate fully loaded staffing costs, relief cover, supervision, equipment, transport, insurance, compliance and overhead.
- [ ] Model the working capital needed to pay operating costs before the customer pays.
- [ ] Establish a minimum acceptable price and margin before quoting.
- [ ] Draft a service-delivery checklist, incident escalation process and client reporting approach.
- [ ] Confirm that staffing and management capacity can meet the proposed contract without relying on unverified assumptions.

**Output:** A costed sample contract model and a realistic delivery plan.

## Priority 5 — Founder terms and lean setup

- [ ] Prepare a non-binding founder term sheet for review.
- [ ] Agree ownership principles, responsibilities, decision rights, expense approval, compensation, IP ownership/licensing, departure and dispute handling.
- [ ] Get appropriate South African legal/accounting advice before binding commitments.
- [ ] Only after alignment, proceed with authorised company setup, brand, domain, business email and a simple website.
- [ ] Keep Rosscore's reusable tools and IP separate unless a written agreement says otherwise.
- [ ] Do not build bespoke software until a real, recurring business problem justifies it.

**Output:** Clear founder terms and only the setup assets needed for the next business milestone.
