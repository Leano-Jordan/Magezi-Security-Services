# Magezi Security Services — Initial Action Plan

**Status:** Proposed actions; owners and deadlines must be confirmed.  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20  
**Operating rule:** Business planning first; software development only when a validated need exists.

## Priority 1 — Establish a fair founder arrangement

Do not decide Rosco's title or ownership percentage before understanding both sides' contributions and expectations.

- [ ] Have a focused discussion with the prospective operating partner about his goals, expectations and what he wants Rosco to take responsibility for.
- [ ] Write down each person's expected work: weekly time, responsibilities, skills, relationships, equipment and ongoing commitments.
- [ ] Ask what cash is actually available, what it must cover, who can pay which costs, and whether either person expects repayment.
- [ ] Separate ownership from payment for work: discuss whether Rosco's work is compensated through equity, fees, salary later, or a clearly defined combination.
- [ ] Compare fair structures rather than jumping to a 50/50 split: equal co-founders; unequal equity based on risk and contribution; equity earned over time/milestones; or a paid-services arrangement plus a smaller stake. These are options to evaluate, not decisions.
- [ ] Agree how to value ongoing work, cash contributions, expenses, decision-making, and what happens if one person stops contributing.
- [ ] Define a limit for unpaid work and spending until written terms are agreed.
- [ ] Check the partner's current employment obligations and any conflicts before approaching clients or using employer resources.

**Output:** A short founder discussion note with proposed contributions, preferred arrangement, unresolved questions and the next decision. No ownership percentage needs to be decided at the first meeting.

## Priority 2 — Check feasibility and compliance

- [ ] Obtain the current PSiRA business-registration checklist directly from PSiRA.
- [ ] Verify the prospective operating partner's individual registration, grade, standing and relevant qualifications.
- [ ] Confirm whether proposed directors and people performing executive/management functions must meet specific PSiRA registration requirements.
- [ ] Before deciding whether Rosco should be a director, confirm the regulatory implications. Shareholding and directorship are not the same thing.
- [ ] Confirm CIPC, SARS, payroll, UIF, COIDA, insurance, employment and premises requirements relevant to the intended model.
- [ ] Build a list of setup and ongoing costs, marking each as verified, estimated or unknown.
- [ ] Do not commit to costs until the funding gap and likely first-contract economics are understood.

**Output:** A verified compliance checklist with costs, evidence needed, responsible person and status.

## Priority 3 — Test customer demand

- [ ] Ask whether the partner has any real prospects, referrals or potential clients already identified. Record names only where appropriate and with a legitimate business purpose.
- [ ] Select one initial customer segment based on the partner's experience and realistic operating capacity.
- [ ] Build a small list of local businesses/property decision-makers using public business contact channels.
- [ ] Speak with prospective buyers about their guarding needs, contract expectations, procurement requirements, decision process and payment terms.
- [ ] Do not imply Magezi is already licensed, insured or operational if that has not been verified.
- [ ] Record each conversation, evidence, objections, next step and follow-up date.

**Output:** Evidence of demand, a prioritised prospect list and a clear view of what a first customer would require.

## Priority 4 — Prove contract economics and delivery capacity

- [ ] Define the exact duties, hours, number of guards and geographic coverage for a sample contract.
- [ ] Estimate full staffing costs, relief cover, supervision, equipment, transport, insurance, compliance and overhead.
- [ ] Model the working capital needed to pay costs before the client pays.
- [ ] Establish a minimum viable price and margin before quoting.
- [ ] Draft a service-delivery checklist, incident escalation process and client reporting approach.
- [ ] Confirm the business can cover absence, replacement, supervision and late payment without relying on unverified assumptions.

**Output:** A costed sample contract model and a realistic delivery plan.

## Priority 5 — Record terms, then set up only what is needed

- [ ] Prepare a non-binding founder term sheet after both sides have discussed their contributions and preferred arrangement.
- [ ] Cover ownership and how it is earned, roles, decision rights, compensation, expenses, IP, use of Rosscore assets, deadlock, departure and dispute handling.
- [ ] Get appropriate South African legal/accounting advice before binding commitments.
- [ ] Only after alignment, proceed with the appropriate company setup, brand, domain, business email and a simple website.
- [ ] Do not build bespoke software until a real, recurring business problem justifies it.

**Output:** Written founder principles and a lean, justified setup plan.
